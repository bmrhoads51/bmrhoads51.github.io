01 / About

About my work

My research explores how machine learning can connect material microstructures with their properties. In a published study of Ni–Al alloys, my coauthors and I used graph neural networks and explainable AI to predict strengthening during precipitate coarsening and examine which features matter most.

I also develop computer vision projects involving image processing, geometric transformations, object detection, and visual language models. This site will grow as I add more publications and professional work.
